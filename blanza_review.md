The overall repository architecture is cleanly laid out utilizing a standard solution file alongside clear component nesting that makes navigating the codebase straightforward without needing deep context. The ui design really accumulate the sizes of the page which transforms whenever the screen is small enough, but I noticed that when the screen is too small about like 50px x 50px (approx) the screen format is completely destroyed not having the ability to scroll horizontally. Well overall the design is very human, I mean who would be an idiot to use a very small window like that to trigger the thing I mentioned lol
